# PeakPilot Outreach Package

## Objective

Secure discovery conversations and convert qualified facilities into a 30-day free pilot.

Do NOT sell software.

Do NOT sell AI.

Do NOT sell dashboards.

Sell visibility into costly demand spikes before they become electricity charges.

---

# Core Sales Principle

Never start with:

* Software
* AI
* Platform
* Dashboard
* Energy optimization

Always start with a business problem.

The prospect should immediately think:

"Yes, this can cost me money."

---

# Core Positioning

Most facilities discover demand-charge events only after the electricity bill arrives.

By then the money is already gone.

PeakPilot helps identify demand-risk events while they are happening so operators can respond before those events become charges.

---

# Social Proof Hook

Use when appropriate.

"Humne recently Delhi NCR ki multiple HT-connected industrial facilities ke demand patterns study kiye.

Common observation ye tha ki facilities ko demand spike ka exact timing usually bill aane ke baad hi pata chalta hai.

Tab tak charge already lock ho chuka hota hai."

Never overuse this.

Use only once per conversation.
