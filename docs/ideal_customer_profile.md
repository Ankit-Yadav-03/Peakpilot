# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — Phase 1 Market Entry

## Core Positioning

Target facilities that:

* Pay significant demand charges
* Have operational flexibility during peak periods
* Lack real-time visibility into demand-risk events
* Can act on operational recommendations within minutes

The platform is designed to reduce avoidable demand-charge exposure, not overall energy consumption.

---

# Primary Target Industry

## Industrial Cold Storage & Refrigerated Warehousing

Especially facilities operating:

* Frozen storage
* Multi-chamber cold storage
* Food logistics warehouses
* Fruit and vegetable storage
* Dairy cold storage
* Meat and seafood cold storage

---

# Why This Industry

Cold-storage operators routinely experience:

* Large compressor-driven electrical demand
* Demand peaks that occur unexpectedly
* Seasonal electricity-cost pressure
* High fixed demand charges
* Limited visibility into real-time demand-risk events

Most facilities only discover demand-charge problems after receiving the electricity bill.

By then the financial loss has already occurred.

The platform addresses this visibility gap.

# Ideal Facility Characteristics

## Minimum Qualification

* HT electricity connection
* Contract demand above 100 kVA
* Monthly electricity bill above ₹1 lakh
* Digital energy meter available
* Facility operating at least 16 hours per day

---

## Strong Qualification

* Contract demand above 200 kVA
* Monthly electricity bill above ₹3 lakh
* Refrigeration compressors operating continuously
* Multiple refrigeration circuits
* Existing demand-charge component on electricity bill

---

## Best Qualification

* Contract demand above 500 kVA
* Monthly electricity bill above ₹5 lakh
* Multiple compressor banks
* 24×7 operation
* Historical electricity bills available
* Existing concern regarding demand charges

---

# Decision Makers

## Primary Buyer

* Owner
* Managing Director
* Proprietor

These individuals usually feel the direct impact of rising electricity costs.

---

## Secondary Influencers

* Plant Head
* Operations Head
* Facility Manager

These stakeholders typically evaluate operational feasibility.

---

## Internal Data Sources

* Accountant
* Electrical Supervisor
* Maintenance Manager
* Energy Manager (if present)

These individuals usually provide bills and operational information but rarely approve purchases.

---

# Primary Business Problems

## Problem 1

Avoidable demand-charge costs are discovered only after utility billing.

---

## Problem 2

No visibility into developing peak-demand events.

---

## Problem 3

No objective measurement of whether operational actions actually reduced demand risk.

---

## Problem 4

Difficulty identifying which equipment contributes most to demand peaks.

---

## Problem 5

Lack of historical evidence showing where demand-charge losses occur.

---

# Buying Triggers

A prospect becomes significantly more likely to engage when:

* Electricity bills have increased recently
* Demand charges have increased recently
* Facility capacity has expanded
* Additional refrigeration equipment was installed
* Operating margins are under pressure
* Summer season is approaching
* Management is reviewing operational costs

---

# First Conversation Objective

Do not sell software.

Do not discuss AI.

Do not discuss machine learning.

Determine only:

1. Monthly electricity spend
2. Contract demand value
3. Whether demand charges appear on the electricity bill
4. Whether electricity costs are considered a business concern
5. Whether a recent electricity bill can be shared

---

# First Data Request

Only:

* One recent electricity bill

Nothing else.

The objective is to identify demand-charge exposure before discussing deployment.

---

# Qualification Criteria

A qualified prospect:

* Pays meaningful demand charges
* Has operational flexibility
* Has controllable electrical loads
* Can provide one recent electricity bill
* Has access to the decision maker

---

# Disqualification Criteria

Do not spend time on facilities where:

* Monthly electricity spend is insignificant
* Demand charges are negligible
* Electricity cost is not viewed as a business problem
* No decision maker is accessible
* No bill can be shared after initial interest
* Operations have little or no flexibility during peak periods

---

# Success Definition

An ideal prospect should allow you to answer one question:

"Can this facility be shown measurable avoidable demand-charge exposure within 30 days?"

If the answer is yes, the facility is a strong pilot candidate.
