# 30-Day Electricity Cost Leakage Assessment Pilot

## Every Month, Facilities Pay Demand Charges They Never Intended To Pay

Most facilities monitor energy consumption.

Very few know exactly when avoidable demand peaks occur, which loads contribute to those peaks, or how much money is lost because nobody saw the risk early enough.

A single preventable demand spike can increase electricity costs for an entire billing cycle.

The problem is that these events often happen in real time and are only discovered after the utility bill arrives.

By then, the money is already gone.

---

# Purpose of the Pilot

The purpose of this pilot is to determine whether your facility is experiencing avoidable electricity-cost leakage caused by unmanaged demand peaks.

Over 30 days, the platform continuously monitors facility demand behavior and identifies opportunities to reduce future demand-charge exposure.

No operational disruption is required.

No equipment shutdowns are performed automatically.

No changes are made without facility approval.

---

# What We Deploy

We connect to available facility electrical telemetry and configure:

* Contract demand limits
* Tariff structure
* Facility operating profile
* Major electrical loads (where available)

Deployment typically requires minimal operational involvement.

---

# What the Platform Watches For

The platform continuously evaluates:

* Real-time electrical demand
* Demand growth trends
* Maximum Demand Indicator (MDI) risk
* Emerging peak-demand events
* Repeated operational patterns that increase electricity costs

Unlike monthly utility reports, analysis occurs while the event is happening.

---

# What Happens During a Risk Event

When the platform detects a developing demand-risk condition, it generates operational recommendations designed to reduce avoidable demand charges.

Examples include:

* Temporary load reduction opportunities
* Peak-avoidance actions
* Operational sequencing recommendations
* Timing adjustments for non-critical loads

The facility remains in full control.

No automatic control actions are taken.

---

# What We Measure

Throughout the pilot we track:

* Demand-risk events detected
* Recommendations generated
* Operational responses
* Demand reduction achieved
* Compliance rates
* Estimated cost avoidance opportunities

This creates an objective record of how much avoidable demand exposure exists today.

# Pilot Success Criteria

The pilot is considered successful if one or more of the following are identified:

* Preventable demand peaks
* Avoidable demand-charge exposure
* Repeatable operational inefficiencies
* Opportunities for measurable electricity-cost reduction

Even if no savings opportunity is identified, the facility gains objective confirmation that current operations are already well managed.
