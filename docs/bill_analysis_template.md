# bill_analysis_template.md

# Purpose

Convert a prospect's electricity bill into:

1. A quantified savings estimate
2. A clear business case
3. A recommendation for next steps

This process must be repeatable for every prospect.

---

# Stage 1 — Bill Intake

## Required Documents

Minimum:

* Latest electricity bill

Preferred:

* Last 3 bills

Ideal:

* Last 6 bills

Do NOT block analysis if only one bill is available.

---

# Stage 2 — Data Extraction

Create a worksheet and extract:

## Facility Information

* Facility name
* Location
* Utility / DISCOM
* Tariff category
* Billing period

---

## Demand Information

* Contract Demand (kVA)
* Recorded Maximum Demand (kVA)
* Billing Demand (kVA)
* Demand Charge Rate (₹/kVA)

---

## Energy Consumption

* Total Units (kWh)
* Total kVAh
* Average PF
* Energy Charge Amount

---

## Additional Charges

Capture separately:

* Demand Charges
* Energy Charges
* Fixed Charges
* TOD Charges
* TOD Rebates
* PF Incentives
* PF Penalties
* Fuel Adjustment Charges
* Regulatory Charges
* Other Charges

---

## Total Bill

Record:

* Current Bill Amount
* Previous Bill Amount (if available)

---

# Stage 3 — Demand Charge Analysis

## Calculate

Current Monthly Demand Cost

Demand Cost =
Billing Demand × Demand Charge Rate

Example:

250 kVA × ₹450

= ₹112,500/month

---

## Determine Demand Charge Share

Demand Charge %

=
Demand Charges
/
Total Bill

If demand charges represent:

* <10% → weak opportunity
* 10–20% → moderate opportunity
* > 20% → strong opportunity

---

# Stage 4 — Initial Opportunity Scoring

## Opportunity Score

### High

All conditions:

* Demand charge > ₹25,000/month
* Contract demand > 100 kVA
* Continuous operation
* Refrigeration or large motor loads

### Medium

* Demand charge > ₹10,000/month
* Some controllable loads

### Low

* Minimal demand charges
* Small facility
* Limited operational flexibility

---

# Stage 6 — Facility Fit Assessment

## Strong Candidate

* High demand charges
* Compressors
* Chillers
* Pumps
* Refrigeration systems
* 24×7 operations

Recommendation:

Proceed to Pilot

---

## Weak Candidate

* Small demand charges
* Little operational flexibility

Recommendation:

Do Not Pursue

---

# Stage 7 — Pilot Qualification Decision

Proceed only if:

Expected Savings

≥ ₹20,000/month

OR

Annual Opportunity

≥ ₹2,40,000/year

Otherwise deprioritize.
